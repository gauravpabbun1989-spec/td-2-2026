# TicketDepart $0-Ad-Spend Call Acquisition System

## Objective

Grow qualified inbound phone calls without paid advertising.

Funnel:
organic discovery -> high-intent landing page -> phone click -> real call -> qualified call -> revenue

Excluded from this sprint:
- YouTube
- Organic social publishing (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X social-feed campaigns)

Included:
- Google organic search
- Google Business Profile/Maps readiness
- Bing organic search + IndexNow
- Reddit
- Quora
- X/web answers and profile readiness where permitted
- Pinterest/web distribution readiness
- Free business/directories
- Referral and customer-review acquisition
- Email to opted-in contacts
- Travel communities/forums
- Technical SEO and conversion optimization

## Execution rules

1. No paid ads or paid placements.
2. No purchased links, purchased reviews, purchased traffic, or purchased email lists.
3. No automated spam, mass-posting, fake accounts, or deceptive claims.
4. Use the business identity, phone number, address, and disclosures consistently.
5. Community posts must answer the question first; link only when genuinely useful and allowed.
6. Do not claim affiliation with airlines.
7. Do not promise fares, availability, refunds, or savings unless verified for the specific itinerary.
8. Track the funnel: impressions -> clicks -> phone clicks -> calls -> qualified calls -> revenue.

## Free acquisition workstreams

### Google organic
- Maintain high-intent booking pages.
- Strengthen internal links from home, booking guides, airline pages, and route pages.
- Improve titles/descriptions for pages with impressions but low CTR.
- Refresh pages ranking in positions 4-20.
- Keep sitemap and canonical URLs clean.
- Recheck indexing after meaningful changes.

### Google Business Profile / Maps
- Use only if TicketDepart is eligible under Google's current business-profile rules.
- Keep business name, address, category, hours, website, and phone consistent.
- Request genuine customer reviews after legitimate interactions.
- Never create duplicate locations or fake service areas.

### Bing
- Keep sitemap available.
- Keep IndexNow validation healthy.
- Monitor Bing indexing and crawl status.
- Re-submit changed URLs through the existing IndexNow workflow when appropriate.

### Reddit / Quora / travel communities
Use helpful answers around:
- booking a flight by phone
- complicated itineraries
- multi-city travel
- family/group travel
- airline booking questions
- fare/change/cancellation questions

Do not mass-post promotional links. Build useful answers first.

### Free directories
Prioritize legitimate, relevant directories where a free listing is genuinely available. Keep NAP data consistent and remove duplicate/inaccurate profiles.

### Referrals and reviews
- Ask legitimate customers for honest reviews.
- Ask satisfied customers whether they know another traveler who needs help.
- Do not incentivize or manufacture reviews in ways that violate platform rules.

### Opted-in email
Use only permission-based contacts. Send useful travel information and clear booking-assistance CTAs. Do not use purchased lists.

### Pinterest/web distribution
Create useful, evergreen travel-planning resources and link them to relevant TicketDepart pages where the platform permits it.
